Deny, Defend, Depose

In the Luigi Mangione case, the media has focused more on him than on the man he is accused of killing, Brian Thompson. While Brian was a major player in the healthcare industry, the attention has mostly been on Mangione—his life, his reasons, and the movement that has grown around him.

Who Was Brian Thompson?

Brian Thompson, the victim, hasn’t been the main subject of media coverage. As the CEO of UnitedHealthcare, one of the biggest insurance companies in America, Brian played a big role in the healthcare system. Yet, his career and even his family’s response to his death have been pushed to the side. The media has mostly ignored Brian’s life and accomplishments. Instead, the focus has been on Mangione’s motives and actions, which has changed the conversation from Brian’s tragic death to Mangione’s story.

Luigi Mangione: The Anti-Hero or Criminal?

Luigi Mangione was an engineer who had serious health problems and felt angry at the healthcare system. When police arrested him, they found a 3D-printed gun and a note criticizing insurance companies. These details quickly made the media focus on Mangione as either a criminal or a man standing up against greed in healthcare. Online communities have added to this narrative. On social media, forums, and crowdfunding sites, Mangione is seen by some as a hero fighting an unfair system. A legal defense fund for him has raised hundreds of thousands of dollars, and supporters believe he was driven to his actions by the system, not by choice.

The Role of Media Sensationalism

Instead of focusing on Brian Thompson’s life and what his death means, the media has sensationalized Mangione’s story. His medical struggles, education, and even strange rumors about him have become the headline topics. Ironically, these rumors have made his supporters rally behind him even more, believing they are smear campaigns. This case shows how the media often shifts its focus when a crime becomes a big story. The accused person, not the victim, becomes the main focus. Whether because people are drawn to stories of rebellion or because of biases in reporting, the victim’s story is often forgotten.

A Case That Exposes a Broken System

As of March 2025, Mangione is still in jail waiting for his trial. His supporters see him as proof that the healthcare system is broken, while Brian Thompson’s life and impact remain mostly ignored. The Luigi Mangione case is a clear example of how the media can change public opinion. It raises the question of who deserves to be remembered and what that says about society’s values.
