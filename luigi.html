The Luigi Mangione Case: When the Alleged Killer Becomes the Story

In stark contrast to how the media framed the case of Selena Quintanilla, the coverage of Luigi Mangione has focused overwhelmingly on him rather than the man he is accused of murdering. While the victim, Brian Thompson, was a powerful figure in the healthcare industry, the media's fixation has been on Mangione—his past, his motives, and the movement that has emerged around him.

Who Was Brian Thompson?

Despite being the victim, Brian Thompson has not been the primary focus of media coverage. As the CEO of UnitedHealthcare, he led one of the largest insurance companies in the U.S., playing a significant role in the country's healthcare system. Yet, his career, contributions, and even his family’s response to his death have largely been overshadowed.

Luigi Mangione: The Anti-Hero or Criminal?

Luigi Mangione, a former engineer with chronic health conditions, had long expressed frustration with the American healthcare system. When he was arrested, police found a 3D-printed gun and a note criticizing insurance companies. These details quickly shifted media attention from Thompson’s murder to Mangione’s grievances, with many portraying him as either a cold-blooded criminal or a vigilante fighting against corporate greed.

The Role of Media Sensationalism

Rather than centering the discussion on Thompson’s life and the impact of his murder, the media has largely sensationalized Mangione’s personal history. Stories about his education, medical struggles, and alleged motivations have dominated headlines, sometimes even straying into the bizarre—such as rumors about explicit videos featuring him.

A Case That Exposes a Broken System

As of March 2025, Mangione remains incarcerated, awaiting trial. His supporters continue to rally around him, viewing his actions as a symptom of a broken system rather than an isolated crime. Meanwhile, Brian Thompson’s legacy remains largely unexplored, a footnote in a story that has become more about the accused than the victim.
